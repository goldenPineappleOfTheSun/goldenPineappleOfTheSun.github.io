Privacy Policy

App: Midnight — Blue Light Filter
Developer: Tair Saetov
Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Midnight is an Android application that provides a screen color filter and additional screen dimming for comfortable nighttime use.

We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the collection and processing of personal information.

2. Data Collection

Midnight does not require users to create an account.

The application is designed to operate locally on your device. It does not intentionally collect, transmit, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

The application does not include advertising or analytics services.

3. Local Data Storage

Midnight stores filter preferences and timer settings locally on your device.

These settings are used to remember your preferences and operate the application's features.

The developer does not receive these settings.

4. Permissions

Midnight uses certain Android permissions to provide its functionality:

  • Display over other apps: Allows the screen filter to appear over other applications.
  • Foreground service: Allows the filter to remain active while using other applications.
  • Notifications: Allows the application to display an active filter notification with an option to turn off the filter.
  • Alarms: Allows scheduled activation and deactivation of the screen filter.
  • Device startup: Allows the application to restore scheduled timer behavior after a device restart.

Midnight does not use these permissions to read the contents of other applications or record the screen.

5. Data Sharing

Midnight does not intentionally transmit user information to external servers or share it with third parties.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Application preferences remain stored locally on your device until they are cleared or the application is uninstalled, subject to Android's backup and restore behavior.

You can remove locally stored preferences through your device's application settings.

7. Security

Midnight is designed to process its settings locally without sending them to developer-controlled servers.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change.

Any updates will be published on this page.
